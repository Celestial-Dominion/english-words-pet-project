=== future-in-the-past
sum: Nhìn tương lai từ một mốc quá khứ. Was / were going to + V = đã định (nhưng thường không làm): I was going to call you, but my phone died. Would + V = will của quá khứ, hay đứng sau said, thought, knew, promised: You said you would be home by nine. Was about to + V = sắp sửa… thì: I was about to leave when it started to rain. Tiếng Việt: “đã định…”, “cứ tưởng sẽ…”, “vừa định… thì…”. Để nói “định mà không làm”, dùng was going to (would ở đây dễ bị hiểu là giả định).
form: was / were going to + V = đã định (nhưng…) | I was going to call you, but…
form: would + V = sẽ, nhìn từ quá khứ | I knew you would like it.
form: was about to + V = sắp sửa… thì | I was about to leave when it started to rain.
form: said / thought / knew / promised + would | She said she would come.
cast: mom zoe
bg: home-evening
words: promise, instead, borrow, charge, abroad, give
gloss: charge

#hook
mom: Zoe, why didn't you call me? I was worried! | Zoe, sao con không gọi cho mẹ? Mẹ lo lắm! [worried]
zoe: Sorry, Mom. I was going to call you, but my phone died. | Con xin lỗi mẹ. Con định gọi, nhưng điện thoại hết pin. [shy]
mom: You said you would be home by nine. | Con bảo con sẽ về trước chín giờ mà. [angry]
zoe: I know. I was about to leave when it started to rain. | Con biết. Con vừa định về thì trời đổ mưa. [sad]
> Ba cách nhìn tương lai từ quá khứ. [[I was going to call you]]: đã định gọi, nhưng rồi không gọi. [[You said you would be home]]: lời hứa trong quá khứ, [[will]] lùi thành [[would]]. [[I was about to leave]]: sắp sửa về thì có chuyện xảy ra.

#meaning
tl: -2 was going to call | định gọi
tl: -1 phone died | điện thoại hết pin
tl: now | bây giờ
> Kế hoạch có từ trước, nhưng một việc khác xen vào. Tiếng Việt nói “con định…”, tiếng Anh dùng [[was going to]], và theo sau thường là [[but]].

#form
th: Mẫu | Nghĩa | Ví dụ
tb: was going to + V | đã định (thường không làm) | We were going to go out, but it rained.
tb: would + V | sẽ, nhìn từ quá khứ | I knew you would like it.
tb: was about to + V | sắp sửa… thì | The movie was about to start.
> [[Would]] ở đây không phải giả định, mà là [[will]] của quá khứ. Nó hay đứng sau [[said]], [[thought]], [[knew]], [[hoped]], [[promised]].

#examples
- I {was going to|k} buy a new phone, but it was too expensive. | Tôi đã định mua điện thoại mới, nhưng đắt quá.
- I never thought I {would|k} live abroad. | Tôi chưa bao giờ nghĩ mình sẽ sống ở nước ngoài.
- We {were about to|k} give up when we found the answer. | Chúng tôi vừa định bỏ cuộc thì tìm ra đáp án.
- He promised he {would|k} help us. | Anh ấy đã hứa sẽ giúp chúng tôi.

#natural
rank: best | I was going to call you. | Tôi định gọi cho bạn.
rank: ok | I was planning to call you. | Tôi đã tính gọi cho bạn.
rank: odd | I would call you, but my phone died.
> Để nói “định… nhưng không”, [[was going to]] là tự nhiên nhất. Dùng [[would]] ở đây nghe lạ, vì [[I would call you]] dễ bị hiểu thành giả định: “tôi sẽ gọi nếu…”.

#mistakes
x: I was go to call you. => I was going to call you. | Tôi định gọi cho bạn. >> Cấu trúc đầy đủ: [[was going to]].
x: She said she will come, but she didn't. => She said she would come, but she didn't. | Cô ấy nói sẽ đến, nhưng rồi không đến. >> Nhìn từ quá khứ, [[will]] lùi thành [[would]].
x: I was about leaving when you called. => I was about to leave when you called. | Tôi vừa định đi thì bạn gọi. >> Sắp sửa: [[was about to]] cộng nguyên thể.

#dialogue
mom: Next time, borrow a friend's phone. | Lần sau thì mượn điện thoại của bạn nhé. [explain]
zoe: I was going to do that, but everyone had left. | Con định làm vậy, nhưng mọi người về hết rồi. [sad]
mom: OK. I thought you would be at Anna's house. | Thôi được. Mẹ cứ tưởng con ở nhà Anna. [thinking]
zoe: I'm sorry, Mom. I promise I'll charge my phone next time. | Con xin lỗi mẹ. Con hứa lần sau sẽ sạc pin đầy đủ. [shy]

#recall
? Nói bằng tiếng Anh: Tôi định đi bơi, nhưng trời lạnh quá. => I was going to go swimming, but it was too cold. | Tôi định đi bơi, nhưng trời lạnh quá.
? Nói bằng tiếng Anh: Tôi biết là bạn sẽ thích nó mà. => I knew you would like it. | Tôi biết là bạn sẽ thích nó mà.

#recap
> Nhìn tương lai từ quá khứ: [[was going to]] là đã định, nhưng thường không làm. [[Would]] là [[will]] của quá khứ, hay theo sau [[said]], [[knew]], [[thought]]. Còn [[was about to]] là sắp sửa làm thì có chuyện xen vào.

#practice
fill: I ___ call you, but I forgot. | was going to* / will / am going to || Đã định (nhưng không) → was going to.
fill: She told me she ___ be late. | would* / will / is || Tường thuật quá khứ → would.
fill: The train was ___ leave when we arrived. | about to* / going / about || Sắp sửa → was about to.
fix: He promised he will help me. | He promised he would help me.* / He promised he helps me. / He promised he would helped me. || Promised (quá khứ) → would.
choice: “I was going to buy it, but…” cho biết: | Đã định mua nhưng không mua* / Đã mua rồi / Chắc chắn sẽ mua || Was going to = định nhưng không.
type: I never thought I ___ live in London. | would || Thought (quá khứ) → would.
order: I / was / about / to / leave / when / you / called. | Tôi vừa định đi thì bạn gọi.
listen: Sorry, Mom. I was going to call you, but my phone died. | Sorry, Mom. I was going to call you, but my phone died.* / Sorry, Mom. I'm going to call you, but my phone died. / Sorry, Mom. I was going to call you, but I forgot. || Nghe was going to … phone died.
