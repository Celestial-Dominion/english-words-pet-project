=== comparison-advanced
sum: So sánh nâng cao. Gấp bao nhiêu lần: twice / three times as + tính từ + as (Hanoi is twice as big as Da Nang) hoặc three times + so sánh hơn + than (three times more expensive than). Kém xa: nowhere near as / not nearly as + tính từ + as (not nearly as expensive). Gần bằng: almost / nearly as … as. Nhấn so sánh nhất: by far the best, easily the best (hơn hẳn). The same … as, similar to, a lot like cho sự giống. Không nói twice bigger than trong văn chuẩn.
form: twice / three times as + tính từ + as | twice as big as
form: nowhere near as / not nearly as + tính từ + as | nowhere near as good as
form: by far / easily + so sánh nhất | by far the best
form: almost / nearly as … as | nearly as tall as his dad
cast: nam ben
bg: cafe
words: twice, nearly, expensive, population, crowded, rent
gloss: rent, sequel

#hook
ben: How was Tokyo? Is it as big as Hanoi? | Tokyo thế nào? Có to bằng Hà Nội không? [thinking]
nam: Much bigger! Its population is about three times as large as Hanoi's. | To hơn nhiều! Dân số gấp khoảng ba lần Hà Nội. [explain]
ben: Wow. Is it expensive? | Chà. Có đắt đỏ không? [surprised]
nam: Very. But the food is by far the best I've ever had. | Rất đắt. Nhưng đồ ăn thì ngon nhất mà tôi từng ăn, hơn hẳn. [happy]
ben: Better than here? | Ngon hơn ở đây à? [thinking]
nam: Hmm, our coffee is still twice as good! | Hừm, cà phê của mình vẫn ngon gấp đôi! [happy]
> [[Three times as large as]]: gấp ba lần. [[Twice as good]]: gấp đôi. [[By far the best]]: tốt nhất, hơn hẳn phần còn lại. Đây là những cách so sánh chính xác và giàu cảm xúc hơn so với [[bigger]], [[better]] đơn thuần.

#form
th: Mẫu | Nghĩa | Ví dụ
tb: twice as … as | gấp đôi | twice as big as
tb: three times as … as | gấp ba | three times as expensive as
tb: nowhere near as … as | kém xa | nowhere near as good as
tb: almost / nearly as … as | gần bằng | nearly as tall as
tb: by far the … | nhất, hơn hẳn | by far the best
> Muốn nói gấp bao nhiêu lần, đặt [[twice]], [[three times]] trước [[as … as]]. Muốn nói kém xa, dùng [[nowhere near as]] hoặc [[not nearly as]].

#examples
- My new apartment is {twice as big as|k} my old one. | Căn hộ mới của tôi rộng gấp đôi căn cũ.
- The sequel was {nowhere near as good as|n} the first movie. | Phần hai kém xa phần một.
- He's {nearly as tall as|k} his father now. | Giờ cậu ấy cao gần bằng bố rồi.
- She's {by far the best|k} player on the team. | Cô ấy là người chơi giỏi nhất đội, hơn hẳn mọi người.
- Rent in the city center is {three times higher than|k} here. | Tiền thuê nhà ở trung tâm đắt gấp ba ở đây.

#natural
rank: best | It's twice as big as my old room. | Nó rộng gấp đôi phòng cũ của tôi.
rank: ok | It's two times bigger than my old room. | Nó to hơn phòng cũ gấp hai lần.
rank: odd | It's twice bigger than my old room.
> Với “gấp đôi”, [[twice as … as]] là chuẩn và tự nhiên nhất. [[Two times bigger]] vẫn nghe thấy khi nói chuyện. Còn [[twice bigger]] thì lạ tai, nên tránh.

#contrast
vs: The subway is much faster than the bus. | Tàu điện ngầm nhanh hơn xe buýt nhiều. || so sánh hơn + much
vs: The bus is nowhere near as fast as the subway. | Xe buýt chậm hơn tàu điện ngầm rất xa. || nowhere near as … as
> Hai câu cùng một ý, nhưng nhìn từ hai phía. [[Nowhere near as fast as]] nhấn mạnh khoảng cách rất lớn, mạnh hơn [[not as fast as]].

#mistakes
x: It's twice bigger than my car. => It's twice as big as my car. | Nó to gấp đôi xe của tôi. >> Gấp đôi: [[twice as big as]].
x: She's by far best student. => She's by far the best student. | Cô ấy là học sinh giỏi nhất, hơn hẳn. >> So sánh nhất luôn có [[the]]: [[by far the best]].
x: This phone is nowhere near as good than mine. => This phone is nowhere near as good as mine. | Điện thoại này kém xa cái của tôi. >> Cặp [[as … as]], không dùng [[than]].

#dialogue
ben: Would you go back to Tokyo? | Anh có quay lại Tokyo không? [thinking]
nam: Definitely. The trains there are twice as fast as ours. | Chắc chắn rồi. Tàu ở đó nhanh gấp đôi tàu mình. [happy]
ben: But are the people as friendly as people here? | Nhưng người ở đó có thân thiện bằng người ở đây không? [thinking]
nam: Almost as friendly. But nobody is as friendly as you, Ben! | Gần bằng. Nhưng chẳng ai thân thiện bằng anh đâu, Ben! [happy]

#recall
? Nói bằng tiếng Anh: Căn nhà này rộng gấp đôi nhà tôi. => This house is twice as big as mine. | Căn nhà này rộng gấp đôi nhà tôi.
? Nói bằng tiếng Anh: Phim này kém xa cuốn sách. => The movie is nowhere near as good as the book. | Phim này kém xa cuốn sách.

#recap
> Gấp bao nhiêu lần: [[twice]], [[three times]] cộng [[as … as]]. Kém xa: [[nowhere near as]], [[not nearly as]]. Gần bằng: [[nearly as … as]]. Và nhất, hơn hẳn: [[by far the best]].

#practice
fill: This bag is ___ as heavy as that one. | twice* / two / double || Gấp đôi → twice as … as.
fill: The new phone is nowhere near ___ good as the old one. | as* / so / than || Nowhere near as … as.
fill: She's ___ the fastest runner in the school. | by far* / much / very || Nhấn so sánh nhất → by far.
fix: My house is three times bigger as yours. | My house is three times as big as yours.* / My house is three times big as yours. / My house is three bigger times than yours. || Three times as big as.
choice: “He's nearly as tall as his dad.” nghĩa là: | Cậu ấy cao gần bằng bố* / Cậu ấy cao hơn bố / Cậu ấy thấp hơn bố rất nhiều || Nearly as … as = gần bằng.
type: The sequel was nowhere ___ as good as the first movie. | near || Nowhere near as … as.
order: My / new / apartment / is / twice / as / big / as / my / old / one. | Căn hộ mới của tôi rộng gấp đôi căn cũ.
listen: She's by far the best player on the team. | She's by far the best player on the team.* / She's far from the best player on the team. / She's the best player on the other team. || Nghe by far the best.
