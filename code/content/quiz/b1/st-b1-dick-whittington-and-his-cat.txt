=== st-b1-dick-whittington-and-his-cat 1
? Why did Dick want to go to London? | Vì sao Dick muốn đến London?
+ He heard its streets were paved with gold. | Cậu nghe nói đường phố ở đó lát bằng vàng.
- His parents were living there. | Bố mẹ cậu sống ở đó.
- He had a job waiting for him there. | Có một công việc đang chờ cậu ở đó.
- He wanted to see the king. | Cậu muốn gặp nhà vua.
> Chương 1: «They said that nobody was ever hungry in London, and that the streets were paved with gold.»

? How did Dick travel to London? | Dick đến London bằng cách nào?
+ He walked beside a big wagon. | Cậu đi bộ bên cạnh một cỗ xe lớn.
- He rode on a horse. | Cậu cưỡi ngựa.
- He went by ship. | Cậu đi tàu thủy.
- He travelled with his parents. | Cậu đi cùng bố mẹ.
> Chương 1: «Dick asked the driver if he could walk beside it, and the kind man agreed.»

? What did Dick find in the streets of London? | Dick thấy gì trên đường phố London?
+ Only dirt and mud | Chỉ có bụi đất và bùn
- Streets covered with gold | Những con phố phủ vàng
- Many kind and happy people | Nhiều người tử tế, vui vẻ
- Free food for everyone | Đồ ăn miễn phí cho mọi người
> Chương 1: «But there was only dirt and mud everywhere, and when it grew dark he sat down in a corner and cried himself to sleep.»

=== st-b1-dick-whittington-and-his-cat 2
? Who helped Dick when he lay by the door? | Ai đã giúp Dick khi cậu nằm bên cửa?
+ Mr Fitzwarren, the owner of the house | Ông Fitzwarren, chủ ngôi nhà
- The cook who worked in the kitchen | Bà đầu bếp làm trong bếp
- A gentleman with dirty shoes | Một quý ông đi giày bẩn
- The driver of the wagon | Người đánh xe
> Chương 2: «Just then the owner of the house, a rich merchant called Mr Fitzwarren, came home for dinner.»

? What was the problem with the cook? | Bà đầu bếp có vấn đề gì?
+ She was bad-tempered and very unfair to him. | Bà cáu kỉnh và rất bất công với cậu.
- She never gave him anything to eat. | Bà chẳng bao giờ cho cậu ăn.
- She made him sleep outside in the cold. | Bà bắt cậu ngủ ngoài trời lạnh.
- She did not speak his language. | Bà không nói cùng thứ tiếng với cậu.
> Chương 2: «Dick would have been very happy there, but the cook was bad-tempered and gave him the hardest jobs.»

? Why did Dick buy a cat? | Vì sao Dick mua một con mèo?
+ His room was full of rats and mice. | Phòng cậu đầy chuột.
- He felt lonely in the big house. | Cậu thấy cô đơn trong ngôi nhà lớn.
- Alice asked him to buy one. | Alice nhờ cậu mua một con.
- He wanted to sell it for more money. | Cậu muốn bán nó lấy thêm tiền.
> Chương 2: «But Dick had another problem: he slept in a cold little room at the top of the house, which was full of rats and mice.»

=== st-b1-dick-whittington-and-his-cat 3
? Why did Dick send his cat on the ship? | Vì sao Dick gửi con mèo theo tàu?
+ It was the only thing he had. | Đó là thứ duy nhất cậu có.
- He did not like the cat any more. | Cậu không còn thích con mèo nữa.
- The captain needed a cat for the ship. | Thuyền trưởng cần một con mèo trên tàu.
- The cook told him to send it away. | Bà đầu bếp bảo cậu gửi nó đi.
> Dick nói: «I have nothing in the world» — «except a cat that I bought for a penny»

? Why did Dick run away one morning? | Vì sao một buổi sáng Dick bỏ trốn?
+ The cook was crueller than ever. | Bà đầu bếp còn ác hơn bao giờ hết.
- Mr Fitzwarren had sent him away. | Ông Fitzwarren đã đuổi cậu đi.
- He missed his cat too much. | Cậu nhớ con mèo quá.
- He had found a better job. | Cậu đã tìm được việc tốt hơn.
> Chương 3: «After that the cook was crueller than ever, and she made fun of him for sending his cat to sea.» — «At last Dick could not stand it any longer»

? What made Dick turn round and go back? | Điều gì khiến Dick quay lại?
+ The church bells seemed to sing to him. | Tiếng chuông nhà thờ như đang hát cho cậu nghe.
- Alice ran after him and asked him to stay. | Alice chạy theo xin cậu ở lại.
- He was too tired to walk any further. | Cậu quá mệt không đi tiếp được.
- He remembered that he had left his money. | Cậu nhớ ra mình để quên tiền.
> Chương 3: «Then he heard the church bells of London ringing in the distance, and they seemed to sing to him.»

=== st-b1-dick-whittington-and-his-cat 4
? What problem did the king have at his palace? | Nhà vua gặp vấn đề gì ở cung điện?
+ Rats ate all the food on the table. | Chuột ăn hết thức ăn trên bàn.
- His cooks had all run away. | Tất cả đầu bếp đã bỏ trốn.
- He had no money to buy food. | Ông không có tiền mua thức ăn.
- His ship had been lost at sea. | Tàu của ông bị mất tích trên biển.
> Chương 4: «But as soon as the food was served, an army of rats rushed in and ate everything on the table.»

? What did the king promise if the animal could deal with the rats? | Nhà vua hứa gì nếu con vật xử lý được lũ chuột?
+ To fill the ship with gold | Chất đầy vàng lên tàu
- To make the captain a prince | Phong thuyền trưởng làm hoàng tử
- To give the captain his palace | Tặng thuyền trưởng cung điện
- To send the cat back to London | Gửi con mèo về London
> Nhà vua nói: «Bring her to me, and I will fill your ship with gold!»

? How much did the king pay for the cat? | Nhà vua trả bao nhiêu cho con mèo?
+ Ten times as much as for all the other goods | Gấp mười lần tất cả hàng hóa khác
- Exactly one penny | Đúng một xu
- Half as much as for the other goods | Bằng nửa số hàng hóa khác
- Nothing, because it was a present | Không gì cả, vì đó là quà tặng
> Chương 4: «The king bought everything on the ship, and he paid ten times as much for the cat as for all the rest.»

=== st-b1-dick-whittington-and-his-cat 5
? What did Mr Fitzwarren do with the treasure? | Ông Fitzwarren làm gì với kho báu?
+ He gave all of it to Dick. | Ông trao hết cho Dick.
- He kept half of it for himself. | Ông giữ lại một nửa cho mình.
- He gave it to his daughter. | Ông đưa nó cho con gái.
- He used it to buy a new ship. | Ông dùng nó mua tàu mới.
> Chương 5: «Some people said that such a treasure was too much for a kitchen boy, but Mr Fitzwarren refused to keep a penny of it.»

? What did Dick think at first when he was called? | Lúc đầu Dick nghĩ gì khi được gọi lên?
+ That they were playing a trick on him | Rằng mọi người đang trêu cậu
- That he was going to lose his job | Rằng cậu sắp mất việc
- That his cat had come back | Rằng con mèo đã quay về
- That the cook had told lies about him | Rằng bà đầu bếp đã nói dối về cậu
> Chương 5: «Dick was cleaning pots when he was called, and at first he thought that they were playing a trick on him.»

? What do historians think about the story of the cat? | Các nhà sử học nghĩ gì về chuyện con mèo?
+ They doubt that it is true. | Họ hoài nghi nó có thật.
- They are sure that it happened. | Họ chắc chắn nó đã xảy ra.
- They think the cat belonged to Alice. | Họ nghĩ con mèo là của Alice.
- They believe the king never existed. | Họ tin nhà vua chưa từng tồn tại.
> Chương 5: «Historians doubt the story of the cat, but Londoners still remember him as a generous man who gave much of his wealth to the city.»
