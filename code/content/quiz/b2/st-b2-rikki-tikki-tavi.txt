- A bird dropped him there | Một con chim thả cậu xuống đó
> Chương 1: «One summer, a heavy flood washed him out of the burrow where he lived with his parents and carried him, half drowned, into a garden.»

- Leaving him in the mud | Bỏ cậu lại trong bùn
> Chương 1: «let's take him in and dry him. Perhaps he isn't really dead.»

- Anger | Cơn giận
- Sadness | Nỗi buồn
> Chương 1: «Like every mongoose, he was eaten up with curiosity, and he immediately began to explore the house.»

=== st-b2-rikki-tikki-tavi 3

- Steal the family's food | Ăn trộm thức ăn của gia đình

? Who ended the fight in the bathroom? | Ai đã kết thúc trận chiến trong phòng tắm?

- Teddy's mother | Mẹ của Teddy
- Chuchundra | Chuchundra
- Darzee | Darzee
> Chương 3: «The noise woke the father, who rushed in and ended the fight with his gun.»

=== st-b2-rikki-tikki-tavi 4
? Why did Rikki-tikki have to find the eggs? | Vì sao Rikki-tikki phải tìm những quả trứng?
+ They would hatch into dozens of cobras | Chúng sẽ nở ra hàng chục con rắn hổ mang
- He was very hungry after the fight | Cậu rất đói sau trận chiến
- Teddy wanted them for a collection | Teddy muốn sưu tầm chúng
- Darzee wanted to sit on them | Darzee muốn ấp chúng
> Chương 4: «If those eggs hatch, there will be dozens of little cobras»

+ She pretended to have a broken wing | Cô giả vờ bị gãy cánh

- She called the family for help | Cô gọi gia đình đến giúp
> Chương 4: «She pretended that her wing was broken and fluttered along the ground, crying, until Nagaina followed her away from the nest.»

? Where had Nagaina gone while Rikki-tikki was at the melon bed? | Trong lúc Rikki-tikki ở luống dưa, Nagaina đã đi đâu?

- To her hole | Về hang
- To the river | Ra sông
- To Darzee's nest | Đến tổ của Darzee
> Chương 4: «Nagaina has gone to the house! She is going to kill the family!»
