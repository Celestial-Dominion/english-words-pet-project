> Chương 1: «In the autumn of 1991, the Harcourt Emerald disappeared from a locked cabinet in Harcourt Hall, a grand manor house in the Welsh countryside.» — «The stone had belonged to the Harcourt family for two hundred years, and it was insured for two million pounds.»

? Why did suspicion fall on Nesta? | Vì sao nghi ngờ đổ dồn vào Nesta?
+ She had a key and money problems. | Bà có chìa khoá và gặp khó khăn tiền bạc.
- She had stolen things before. | Bà từng ăn trộm trước đây.
- She left the village that night. | Bà rời làng đêm đó.
- Lady Harcourt accused her. | Phu nhân Harcourt buộc tội bà.
> Chương 1: «She had a key to the cabinet, she had financial problems after her husband's death, and she had been seen near the library late that evening.»

? What happened to Nesta after the theft? | Sau vụ trộm Nesta ra sao?

- She was sent to prison for ten years. | Bà bị tù mười năm.

=== st-b2-the-cold-case 2

- She found new evidence herself. | Bà tự tìm ra bằng chứng mới.
> Chương 2: «But when Theo told her that Nesta Lloyd was still alive, aged ninety-one, and that she had agreed to be interviewed, Gwen fetched her coat.»

=== st-b2-the-cold-case 4
? What did the DNA test show? | Xét nghiệm DNA cho thấy gì?
+ The cigarette matched Julian Harcourt. | Mẩu thuốc khớp với Julian Harcourt.
- The cigarette belonged to the gardener. | Mẩu thuốc là của người làm vườn.
- Nesta had touched the cabinet. | Nesta đã chạm vào tủ.
- The emerald was a fake. | Viên ngọc là giả.
> Chương 4: «The result matched Julian Harcourt, who was now seventy and living in a villa in the south of France.»

? Why had Julian taken the emerald? | Vì sao Julian lấy viên ngọc?
+ He owed a fortune to a gambling club. | Anh ta nợ một câu lạc bộ cờ bạc cả gia tài.
- He wanted to give it to his wife. | Anh ta muốn tặng vợ.
- He hated his aunt. | Anh ta ghét dì.
- He thought it was his. | Anh ta nghĩ nó là của mình.
> Chương 4: «He had owed a fortune to a gambling club, and he had taken the emerald from the cabinet using his aunt's key, which he had copied weeks earlier.»

? Why could Julian not be prosecuted for the theft? | Vì sao Julian không thể bị truy tố tội trộm?
+ Too much time had passed. | Đã quá nhiều thời gian trôi qua.
- He lived in France. | Anh ta sống ở Pháp.
- The emerald was found. | Viên ngọc đã được tìm thấy.
- Nesta forgave him. | Nesta tha thứ cho anh ta.
> Chương 4: «Because of the time that had passed, he could no longer be prosecuted for the theft, but he could be sued, and the story made headlines around the world.»
