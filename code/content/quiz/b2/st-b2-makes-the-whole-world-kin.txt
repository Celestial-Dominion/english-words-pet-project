+ The family had gone away, leaving the husband alone | Gia đình đã đi vắng, chỉ còn người chồng ở nhà
- The owner was the richest man in the town | Chủ nhà là người giàu nhất thị trấn
- The front door had been left open all night | Cửa trước bị bỏ ngỏ suốt đêm
- A friend had told him about a hidden safe | Một người bạn đã kể cho hắn về chiếc két giấu kín
> Chương 1: «The burglar, a careful and experienced professional, had studied it for a week, and he knew that the family had gone to the seaside, leaving the husband alone.»

> Chương 1: «A middle-aged man was lying in bed, reading a book by the light of a small lamp.»

- He jumped out of the window | Ông nhảy qua cửa sổ
- He threw his book at the burglar | Ông ném cuốn sách vào tên trộm
