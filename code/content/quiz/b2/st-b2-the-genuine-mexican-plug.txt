+ He wanted to ride like the young riders in the town. | Anh muốn cưỡi ngựa như những kỵ sĩ trẻ trong thị trấn.
- He needed a horse to travel across the desert. | Anh cần một con ngựa để băng qua sa mạc.
- His job required him to visit distant mines. | Công việc buộc anh phải đến những khu mỏ xa xôi.
- A friend had advised him to buy a cheap one. | Một người bạn khuyên anh mua một con giá rẻ.
> Chương 1: «I had only just learned to tell a horse from a cow, but I was determined to ride like them.»

? What did the auctioneer's horse look like? | Con ngựa của người bán đấu giá trông thế nào?
+ It had as many bumps and corners as a camel. | Nó đầy u bướu và góc cạnh như lạc đà.
- It was tall, white and very elegant. | Nó cao, trắng và rất thanh lịch.
- It was young, small and clearly frightened. | Nó non, nhỏ và rõ ràng đang sợ hãi.
- It was old, thin and could hardly walk. | Nó già, gầy và gần như không đi nổi.
> Chương 1: «While I was still thinking about it, an auctioneer rode through the square on a black horse with as many bumps and corners as a camel.»

? Why was the writer tempted to buy the horse? | Vì sao người viết bị hấp dẫn mua con ngựa?
+ The price was low. | Giá rẻ.
- The horse was beautiful. | Con ngựa đẹp.
- The saddle was golden. | Yên ngựa bằng vàng.
- His friends told him to. | Bạn bè bảo anh mua.
> Chương 1: «It was not a handsome animal, but the price was tempting, and I could hardly resist.»
