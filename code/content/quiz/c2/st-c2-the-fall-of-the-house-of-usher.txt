> Chương 1: «Only a close observer might have noticed a barely perceptible fissure that ran in a zigzag line from the roof down the front wall, until it disappeared into the sullen waters of the tarn.»

> Chương 1: «I had come in answer to a letter from Roderick Usher, a companion of my boyhood, whom I had not seen for many years.»

=== st-c2-the-fall-of-the-house-of-usher 2
? How had Roderick changed? | Roderick đã thay đổi thế nào?
+ Pale, with strange eyes | Tái nhợt, mắt lạ thường
- He had grown fat and lazy | Anh béo ra và lười biếng
- He had become very cheerful | Anh trở nên rất vui vẻ
- He had lost his voice | Anh bị mất giọng
> Chương 2: «He had changed so terribly that I could hardly recognize him: his skin was ghastly pale, his eyes unnaturally bright, and his fine hair floated around his face like a web.»

? What was wrong with Roderick's senses? | Giác quan của Roderick có vấn đề gì?

- He was going blind | Anh đang mù dần

- He was deaf in one ear | Anh bị điếc một tai
> Chương 2: «He suffered, he told me, from a morbid sharpness of the senses: he could bear only the plainest food, only clothes of a certain texture, and only the faintest light.»

? What did Roderick believe about the house? | Roderick tin điều gì về tòa nhà?
+ It influenced his spirit | Nó ảnh hưởng đến tâm hồn anh
- It was full of treasure | Nó chứa đầy kho báu
- It would soon be sold | Nó sắp bị bán
- It had been built by his sister | Nó do em gái anh xây
> Chương 2: «He believed, too, that the house itself, its grey stones, its fungi and its silent tarn, had gradually acquired an influence over his spirit.»

=== st-c2-the-fall-of-the-house-of-usher 3

+ Roderick's sister | Em gái của Roderick
- Roderick's wife | Vợ của Roderick
- A servant in the house | Một người hầu trong nhà
- The narrator's cousin | Em họ của người kể
> Chương 3: «Much of his gloom, Roderick admitted, came from a more natural cause: the long illness of his beloved sister Madeline, his only companion for many years and his last relative on earth.»

- She screamed and ran | Cô la hét và bỏ chạy
- She could not stop laughing | Cô không ngừng cười được
- She forgot who she was | Cô quên mình là ai
> Chương 3: «Her disease had long baffled her doctors: a slow wasting away of the person, and frequent attacks of catalepsy, in which she lay rigid and apparently lifeless for hours.»

? What did Roderick do when Madeline passed through the room? | Roderick làm gì khi Madeline đi qua phòng?
+ He hid his face and wept | Anh che mặt khóc
- He called her name | Anh gọi tên cô
- He followed her out | Anh đi theo cô ra ngoài
- He laughed nervously | Anh cười bồn chồn
> Chương 3: «I watched her with astonishment not unmingled with dread, and when I turned to Roderick, he had buried his face in his hands, and tears were trickling between his thin fingers.»
