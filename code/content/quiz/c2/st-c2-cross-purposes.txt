=== st-c2-cross-purposes 3
? Why is a direct refusal avoided in many Japanese business settings? | Vì sao nhiều bối cảnh kinh doanh Nhật tránh từ chối thẳng?
+ It causes embarrassment to both sides. | Nó gây ngượng ngùng cho cả hai bên.
- It is forbidden by company law. | Luật công ty cấm điều đó.
- It is considered a sign of weakness. | Nó bị coi là dấu hiệu yếu đuối.
- It takes far too much time. | Nó tốn quá nhiều thời gian.
> Chương 3: «Yuki explained, as tactfully as she could, that in many Japanese business settings a direct refusal was considered abrasive, since it caused embarrassment to both sides, and that disagreement was often expressed implicitly, through hesitation, euphemism and what was left unsaid.»

? What is a high-context culture? | Văn hoá ngữ cảnh cao là gì?
+ One where meaning lies largely beyond the words | Văn hoá mà ý nghĩa phần lớn nằm ngoài lời nói
- One where people speak very loudly | Văn hoá mà người ta nói rất to
- One where contracts are extremely long | Văn hoá mà hợp đồng cực kỳ dài
- One where everybody speaks English | Văn hoá mà ai cũng nói tiếng Anh
> Chương 3: «The anthropologist Edward Hall had described such cultures as high-context: much of the meaning of a message lay not in its words but in the relationship and the situation.»

? What did Yuki say silence means? | Yuki nói sự im lặng có nghĩa là gì?
+ Time to think | Thời gian để suy nghĩ
- Full agreement | Sự đồng ý hoàn toàn
- Hidden anger | Sự giận dữ ngấm ngầm
- Simple boredom | Sự chán nản đơn thuần
> Yuki nói: «Silence isn't agreement» — «It's time to think.»

=== st-c2-cross-purposes 4

> Chương 4: «That evening, at Yuki's suggestion, they did something Neil had never done in twenty years of selling: they asked for a second meeting, not to press for an answer, but to understand the objection.»

? What was the real problem? | Vấn đề thực sự là gì?

- The colour of the oak they used | Màu của loại gỗ sồi họ dùng

> Chương 4: «Oak, Mr. Mori explained, behaved unpredictably in the humid summers of Osaka, and a British supplier twelve thousand kilometres away could not guarantee to repair a joint that had loosened.»

- By writing a formal letter of complaint | Bằng cách viết thư khiếu nại chính thức

> Chương 4: «He had tried, in the first meeting, to signal this concern tacitly, by praising the beauty of the tables and saying nothing about their practicality, and he had assumed that Neil understood, never suspecting the discrepancy between what he had said and what Neil had heard.»
