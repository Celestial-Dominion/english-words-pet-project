? How did photography affect painting, according to one explanation? | Theo một cách giải thích, nhiếp ảnh tác động đến hội hoạ thế nào?

- It made painting far more expensive. | Nó khiến hội hoạ đắt đỏ hơn nhiều.
- It forced painters to copy photographs. | Nó buộc hoạ sĩ chép lại ảnh chụp.
- It ended the demand for portraits. | Nó chấm dứt nhu cầu vẽ chân dung.
> Bài đọc: «One common explanation points to photography, invented in the 1830s, which relieved painters of the obligation to record appearances with verisimilitude.»

> Bài đọc: «In 1937, the Nazi regime organized an exhibition of so-called degenerate art in Munich, displaying works by Kandinsky, Paul Klee and others alongside mocking slogans, and it attracted more than two million visitors, far more than an accompanying exhibition of approved art.»
