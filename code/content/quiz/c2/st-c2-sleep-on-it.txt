=== st-c2-sleep-on-it 1
? What had happened twelve hours earlier? | Mười hai giờ trước đã xảy ra chuyện gì?
+ A young patient died during surgery. | Một bệnh nhân trẻ qua đời khi đang phẫu thuật.
- Anselm had been badly injured. | Anselm bị thương nặng.
- The hospital had closed his unit. | Bệnh viện đã đóng cửa khoa của anh.
- Celia had made a serious error. | Celia đã mắc sai lầm nghiêm trọng.
> Chương 1: «Twelve hours earlier, a twenty-six-year-old patient named Daniel Asante had died on the operating table during an emergency repair of his aorta, the main artery carrying blood from the heart.»

? Why was the complication so surprising? | Vì sao biến chứng lại bất ngờ đến vậy?
+ The artery tore where scans showed it was strong. | Động mạch rách ở chỗ phim chụp cho thấy chắc khoẻ.
- The patient had refused all treatment. | Bệnh nhân đã từ chối mọi điều trị.
- The operation had lasted only minutes. | Ca mổ chỉ kéo dài vài phút.
- The equipment had suddenly stopped working. | Thiết bị bỗng ngừng hoạt động.
> Chương 1: «The wall of the artery had torn in a place where, according to every scan, it should have been strong, and within minutes the bleeding had become uncontrollable, spreading inexorably despite everything the team could do.»

? What did Celia see in Anselm's face? | Celia thấy gì trên gương mặt Anselm?
+ A kind of disbelief | Một dạng không thể tin nổi
- Open, bitter anger | Sự giận dữ cay đắng ra mặt
- Calm, quiet relief | Sự nhẹ nhõm lặng lẽ, bình thản
- Deep, weary boredom | Sự chán chường mệt mỏi, sâu sắc
> Chương 1: «not grief, exactly, but a kind of disbelief.»

=== st-c2-sleep-on-it 2
? What had Anselm written? | Anselm đã viết gì?
+ His resignation | Đơn từ chức
- A letter to the family | Thư gửi gia đình bệnh nhân
- A report on the operation | Báo cáo về ca mổ
- A complaint about the scans | Khiếu nại về phim chụp
> Anselm nói: «I've written my resignation»

? Why did Celia avoid saying that it was not his fault? | Vì sao Celia tránh nói đó không phải lỗi của anh?
+ It would sound like patronizing reassurance. | Nó sẽ nghe như lời trấn an kẻ cả.
- She believed that it was his fault. | Cô tin rằng đó là lỗi của anh.
- She was far too tired to speak. | Cô quá mệt để nói.
- The hospital forbade such comments. | Bệnh viện cấm những lời như vậy.
> Chương 2: «She knew him well enough to recognize that arguing would be futile, and that telling him it was not his fault, which she believed, would sound like the kind of patronizing reassurance he despised.»

? Why did Celia distrust a decision made at two in the morning? | Vì sao Celia không tin quyết định đưa ra lúc hai giờ sáng?
+ Exhaustion and shock distort judgement. | Kiệt sức và cú sốc làm méo mó phán đoán.
- Night decisions are never legal. | Quyết định ban đêm không bao giờ hợp pháp.
- Anselm always changed his mind. | Anselm luôn thay đổi ý định.
- She wanted to take his position. | Cô muốn giành vị trí của anh.
> Chương 2: «She also knew that exhaustion and shock were distorting his judgement, and that a decision made at two in the morning after a death, which would be irrevocable once announced, was the opposite of the careful, evidence-based reasoning on which his career had been built.»

=== st-c2-sleep-on-it 4
? What did the post-mortem reveal? | Khám nghiệm tử thi cho thấy gì?
+ A rare congenital weakness in the aorta | Một điểm yếu bẩm sinh hiếm gặp ở động mạch chủ
- A mistake made during the operation | Một sai sót xảy ra trong ca mổ
- A severe allergy to one of the drugs | Dị ứng nặng với một loại thuốc
- An infection caught in the hospital | Nhiễm trùng mắc phải trong bệnh viện
> Chương 4: «The post-mortem had revealed an anatomical abnormality: Daniel had a rare congenital weakness in the wall of his aorta, a condition so unusual that it appeared on none of the scans and had been described only a handful of times in the medical literature.»

? Why did the findings bring Anselm little solace? | Vì sao kết luận chẳng an ủi Anselm được mấy?
+ The patient was still dead. | Bệnh nhân vẫn đã ra đi.
- He disagreed with the reviewers. | Anh không đồng ý với hội đồng.
- He was blamed by the family. | Anh bị gia đình đổ lỗi.
- He lost his job anyway. | Dù sao anh cũng mất việc.
> Anselm nói: «It wasn't my fault» — «But he's still dead.»

? What did Celia think was the hardest thing about their profession? | Celia nghĩ điều khó nhất trong nghề của họ là gì?
+ Some losses cannot be prevented at all. | Có những mất mát hoàn toàn không thể ngăn được.
- The working hours are far too long. | Giờ làm việc quá dài.
- Patients rarely say thank you. | Bệnh nhân hiếm khi cảm ơn.
- Reviews are always unfair to surgeons. | Các buổi xem xét luôn bất công với bác sĩ mổ.
> Chương 4: «The hardest thing about their profession, she had come to believe, was not the possibility of error but the certainty that some losses could not be prevented at all.»
