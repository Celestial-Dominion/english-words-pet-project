=== st-c2-to-build-a-fire 1
? What was the man's main weakness? | Điểm yếu chính của người đàn ông là gì?
+ He lacked imagination | Anh thiếu trí tưởng tượng
- He was lazy | Anh lười biếng
- He was afraid of the dark | Anh sợ bóng tối
- He could not read maps | Anh không đọc được bản đồ
> Chương 1: «The trouble with him was that he was without imagination: he was quick and alert in the things of life, but only in the things, and not in their significance.»

? How did the man know it was colder than fifty below? | Làm sao người đàn ông biết trời lạnh hơn âm năm mươi độ?
+ His spit crackled | Nước bọt kêu lách tách
- He had a thermometer | Anh có nhiệt kế
- The dog told him | Con chó báo cho anh
- The river was frozen | Con sông đóng băng
> Chương 1: «When he spat, his spittle crackled in the air before it could fall to the snow. He knew from this that it was colder than fifty below, though how much colder he did not know.»

? Why did he carry his lunch under his shirt? | Vì sao anh mang bữa trưa dưới áo?
+ To stop it from freezing | Để nó khỏi đông cứng
- To hide it from the dog | Để giấu con chó
- Because he had no bag | Vì anh không có túi
- To keep his hands free | Để rảnh tay
> Chương 1: «His lunch, a few biscuits wrapped in a handkerchief, lay securely against his bare skin under his shirt; it was the only way to keep them from freezing.»

=== st-c2-to-build-a-fire 2
? How did the dog feel about travelling that day? | Con chó cảm thấy thế nào về chuyến đi hôm đó?
+ It knew it was too cold | Nó biết trời quá lạnh
- It was excited to run | Nó háo hức được chạy
- It wanted to hunt | Nó muốn đi săn
- It did not notice the cold | Nó không để ý cái lạnh
> Chương 2: «The animal was depressed by the tremendous cold; it knew that it was no time for travelling, and its instinct told it a truer tale than the man's judgment told him.»

? What was the real temperature? | Nhiệt độ thực là bao nhiêu?
+ Seventy-five below | Âm bảy mươi lăm độ
- Fifty below zero | Âm năm mươi độ
- Ten below zero | Âm mười độ
- Just below zero | Chỉ dưới không độ một chút
> Chương 2: «In reality, it was not merely colder than fifty below; it was colder than sixty below, colder than seventy below. It was seventy-five below zero.»

? What was the relationship between the man and the dog? | Quan hệ giữa người đàn ông và con chó thế nào?
+ Master and worker only | Chỉ là chủ và kẻ làm
- They were close friends | Họ là bạn thân
- The dog belonged to the old-timer | Con chó là của ông già
- He had just bought it | Anh vừa mua nó
> Chương 2: «There was no friendship between the dog and the man; the one was the toil-slave of the other, and the only caresses it had ever received were the caresses of the whip.»

=== st-c2-to-build-a-fire 3
? What danger was hidden under the snow? | Mối nguy nào ẩn dưới tuyết?
+ Springs of running water | Mạch nước đang chảy
- Deep holes full of rocks | Hố sâu đầy đá
- Sleeping bears | Gấu đang ngủ
- Old traps set by hunters | Bẫy cũ của thợ săn
> Chương 3: «The creek he followed was frozen solid, but he knew that, even in the coldest weather, unsuspected springs bubbled out from the hillsides and ran under the snow, on top of the ice.»

? What had the old-timer warned him? | Ông già đã cảnh báo anh điều gì?
+ Never to travel alone | Đừng bao giờ đi một mình
- Not to take the dog | Đừng mang theo chó
- Not to eat before dark | Đừng ăn trước khi trời tối
- Not to follow the creek | Đừng đi theo con lạch
> Chương 3: «He remembered the old-timer on Sulphur Creek who had warned him that no man should travel alone in that country when it was colder than fifty below.»

? How did the man react to the warning? | Người đàn ông phản ứng thế nào với lời cảnh báo?
+ He laughed at it | Anh cười nhạo nó
- He turned back | Anh quay lại
- He waited for a friend | Anh chờ một người bạn
- He prayed | Anh cầu nguyện
> Chương 3: «He had laughed at the old man then, and he laughed again now, alone in the white silence, for nothing could be easier than this.»

=== st-c2-to-build-a-fire 4
? Why did the man need to build a fire? | Vì sao người đàn ông cần nhóm lửa?
+ His feet were wet | Chân anh bị ướt
- He was hungry | Anh đói
- It was getting dark | Trời đang tối
- He saw a wolf | Anh thấy một con sói
> Chương 4: «At seventy-five below, wet feet meant a delay of an hour, for he would have to build a fire and dry his socks and moccasins, or lose his toes to frostbite.»

? What was his mistake in building the first fire? | Sai lầm của anh khi nhóm đống lửa đầu tiên là gì?
+ It was under a tree | Nó ở dưới gốc cây
- He used wet wood | Anh dùng củi ướt
- He used all his matches | Anh dùng hết diêm
- He built it on the ice | Anh nhóm nó trên băng
> Chương 4: «It was his own fault, however, that he had built the fire under a spruce tree, whose heavily laden branches hung directly above it.»

? What put out the first fire? | Điều gì làm tắt đống lửa đầu tiên?
+ Falling snow | Tuyết rơi xuống
- A sudden wind | Một cơn gió bất chợt
- The dog's paws | Chân con chó
- Water from the creek | Nước từ con lạch
> Chương 4: «The snow fell on the branches beneath, which capsized in turn. The whole mass descended without warning upon the man and the fire, and the fire was blotted out.»

=== st-c2-to-build-a-fire 5
? Why could he not light a single match? | Vì sao anh không quẹt nổi một que diêm?
+ His fingers were too numb | Ngón tay anh quá tê
- The matches were wet | Diêm bị ướt
- He had lost the matches | Anh làm mất diêm
- The wind blew them out | Gió thổi tắt chúng
> Chương 5: «He began to build a second fire in the open. But his fingers were now so numb that he could not separate one match from the others in the sheaf.»

? What did he plan to do to the dog? | Anh định làm gì con chó?
+ Kill it for warmth | Giết nó để sưởi ấm
- Send it for help | Sai nó đi tìm người giúp
- Tie it to a tree | Buộc nó vào cây
- Ride on its back | Cưỡi lên lưng nó
> Chương 5: «He had a wild idea: he would kill the dog and bury his hands in its warm body until the feeling returned, and then he could build another fire.»

? Why did he let the dog go? | Vì sao anh thả con chó ra?
+ His hands were useless | Tay anh vô dụng
- He felt sorry for it | Anh thấy thương nó
- It bit his arm | Nó cắn tay anh
- He heard his friends | Anh nghe tiếng bạn bè
> Chương 5: «When at last he caught it in his arms, he found that he could neither draw his knife nor close his hands around its throat, and he let it go.»

=== st-c2-to-build-a-fire 6
? How did the man feel at the end? | Cuối cùng người đàn ông cảm thấy thế nào?
+ Strangely calm and sleepy | Bình thản và buồn ngủ lạ lùng
- Angry at the dog | Giận con chó
- Full of energy | Tràn đầy năng lượng
- Terrified until the last moment | Kinh hoàng đến giây phút cuối
> Chương 6: «He had a strange calm now, a kind of drowsiness that seemed almost pleasant; the numbness had crept up his legs and was spreading through his body.»

? What did he say to the old-timer in his mind? | Trong đầu anh nói gì với ông già?
+ "You were right." | "Ông nói đúng."
- "You were wrong." | "Ông sai rồi."
- "Help me." | "Cứu tôi với."
- "Forgive me." | "Tha lỗi cho tôi."
> Chương 6: «"You were right, old man," he mumbled to the old-timer of Sulphur Creek. "You were right."»

? What did the dog do at the end? | Cuối cùng con chó làm gì?
+ It went to the camp | Nó chạy về khu trại
- It stayed by the man | Nó ở lại bên người đàn ông
- It ran into the forest | Nó chạy vào rừng
- It found the old-timer | Nó tìm thấy ông già
> Chương 6: «A little longer it delayed, howling under the stars that leaped and danced in the cold sky, and then it turned and trotted up the trail toward the camp, where there were other food providers and other fire providers.»
