- In the apartment downstairs | Ở căn hộ tầng dưới
- At the shops | Ở cửa hàng
- On holiday | Đi nghỉ
> Chương 1: «Their parents were at work until ten o'clock.»

- She was very cold. | Cô bé rất lạnh.
- She wanted to go out. | Cô bé muốn ra ngoài.
- She could not find the torch. | Cô bé không tìm thấy đèn pin.
> Chương 1: «Nina was frightened of the dark, so she held his hand.»

+ The power cut was very big. | Vụ mất điện rất lớn.
- He lived very far away. | Anh ấy sống rất xa.
- It was snowing heavily. | Tuyết rơi dày.
- He had no torch. | Anh ấy không có đèn pin.
> Chương 2: «They said an engineer would come, but it could take two hours because the power cut was very big.»

=== st-a2-the-night-the-lights-went-out 4

+ At half past nine | Lúc chín giờ rưỡi
- At ten o'clock | Lúc mười giờ
- At midnight | Lúc nửa đêm

> Chương 4: «At half past nine, the engineer arrived and opened the elevator.»

? What did Mrs. Weiss say about the night? | Bà Weiss nói gì về đêm hôm đó?
+ She met her neighbors for the first time. | Lần đầu bà được gặp hàng xóm.
- It was the worst night of her life. | Đó là đêm tệ nhất đời bà.
- She wanted to move to another building. | Bà muốn chuyển sang toà nhà khác.
- She was angry with the engineer. | Bà giận thợ kỹ thuật.
> Bà Weiss nói: «"I've lived here for thirty years, and tonight I met my neighbors for the first time."»

? What do the neighbors do every month now? | Giờ hằng tháng hàng xóm làm gì?
+ They have dinner together. | Họ ăn tối cùng nhau.
- They turn off the lights. | Họ tắt hết đèn.
- They fix the elevator. | Họ sửa thang máy.
- They play football. | Họ đá bóng.
> Chương 4: «Now, on the first Saturday of every month, the neighbors have dinner together.»
