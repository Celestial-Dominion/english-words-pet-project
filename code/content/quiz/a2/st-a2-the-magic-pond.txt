=== st-a2-the-magic-pond 1
? What did some children say to Shreya? | Vài đứa trẻ nói gì với Shreya?
+ She looked like a circus clown. | Trông cô bé như chú hề rạp xiếc.
- She was the best painter in class. | Cô bé là người vẽ giỏi nhất lớp.
- She had beautiful eyes. | Cô bé có đôi mắt đẹp.
- She was very tall. | Cô bé rất cao.
> Chương 1: «"You look like a circus clown!" they said.»

? What did Shreya's mother do? | Mẹ Shreya làm nghề gì?
+ She was a painter. | Bà là hoạ sĩ.
- She was a teacher. | Bà là giáo viên.
- She was a doctor. | Bà là bác sĩ.
- She was a clown. | Bà là chú hề.
> Chương 1: «Her mother was a painter, and every day she taught Shreya to draw something new.»

? Why didn't Shreya want to draw? | Vì sao Shreya không muốn vẽ?
+ A girl had made her cry at school. | Một bạn gái đã làm cô bé khóc ở trường.
- She did not like elephants. | Cô bé không thích voi.
- She was very hungry. | Cô bé rất đói.
- Her father was late. | Bố về muộn.
> Chương 1: «A girl called Roshni had made her cry at school.»

=== st-a2-the-magic-pond 2
? Where did Shreya stop? | Shreya dừng lại ở đâu?
+ At the pond | Ở bờ ao
- At Rina Aunty's house | Ở nhà cô Rina
- At school | Ở trường
- At the market | Ở chợ
> Chương 2: «She stopped only when she reached the pond.»

? What did Shreya think when the bird flew away? | Khi chú chim bay đi, Shreya nghĩ gì?
+ Even the bird didn't like her. | Đến con chim cũng chẳng thích mình.
- The bird was afraid of the water. | Con chim sợ nước.
- The bird was going home. | Con chim đang bay về nhà.
- The bird wanted some food. | Con chim muốn tìm thức ăn.
> Chương 2: «"Even the bird doesn't like me," she thought.»

? What did Shreya put under her head? | Shreya gối đầu lên gì?
+ Her sweater | Chiếc áo len
- Her shoes | Đôi giày
- Her bag | Chiếc cặp
- Some grass | Ít cỏ
> Chương 2: «She put her sweater under her head, and a few minutes later she was fast asleep.»

=== st-a2-the-magic-pond 3
? What did the voice say? | Giọng nói nói gì?
+ "You are beautiful." | "Cháu thật xinh đẹp."
- "Go home now, child." | "Về nhà ngay đi cháu."
- "Don't cry, little girl." | "Đừng khóc, cô bé."
- "I am very hungry." | "Ta đói quá."
> Chương 3: «"You are beautiful. Did you know that?"»

? Who was in the water? | Ai ở dưới nước?
+ A little fairy | Một nàng tiên nhỏ
- A big fish | Một con cá to
- Roshni | Roshni
- Shreya's mother | Mẹ của Shreya
> Chương 3: «There was a little fairy in the water, and she looked very friendly.»

? What did Shreya think about her face in the water? | Shreya nghĩ gì về gương mặt mình dưới nước?
+ Her eyes and nose were nice. | Mắt và mũi khá xinh.
- She looked like a clown. | Trông như chú hề.
- Her face was very sad. | Gương mặt rất buồn.
- She needed new glasses. | Cô bé cần kính mới.
> Shreya nói: «"My eyes are not so small," she said. "And my nose is quite nice."»

=== st-a2-the-magic-pond 4
? What did her teacher say the next day? | Hôm sau cô giáo nói gì?

- Shreya should draw more. | Shreya nên vẽ nhiều hơn.
- Shreya talked too much. | Shreya nói quá nhiều.
> Cô giáo nói: «"I'm glad to see you smiling, Shreya," said her teacher.»

- The water | Mặt nước
- The fairy | Nàng tiên
- New clothes | Quần áo mới
> Chương 4: «The smile made the whole face beautiful.»

? What did Shreya keep as a secret? | Shreya giữ bí mật điều gì?
+ The magic pond | Cái ao thần
- Her drawings | Những bức vẽ
- Roshni's name | Tên của Roshni
- Her new friend | Người bạn mới
> Chương 4: «But the magic pond was Shreya's secret.»
