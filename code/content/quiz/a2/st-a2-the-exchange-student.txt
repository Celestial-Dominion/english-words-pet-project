=== st-a2-the-exchange-student 3

- The flowers were too expensive. | Hoa quá đắt.
- Her grandmother did not like yellow. | Bà cô không thích màu vàng.

? What did the grandmother say about customs? | Bà nói gì về phong tục?

- Flowers are not important. | Hoa không quan trọng.
- Lucas should not buy flowers. | Lucas không nên mua hoa.
> Bà nói: «"The same flower can mean happiness in one country and sadness in another."»
