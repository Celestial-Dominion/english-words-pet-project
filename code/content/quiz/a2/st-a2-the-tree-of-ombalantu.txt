> Chương 1: «Yes, but it is not as old or as big as the tree at Ombalantu»

? Why did Angula run away from his sister? | Vì sao Angula chạy khỏi chỗ chị gái?
+ He ran after a hare. | Cậu đuổi theo một con thỏ rừng.
- He was afraid of the water. | Cậu sợ nước.
- He wanted to go home. | Cậu muốn về nhà.
- He saw some strange men. | Cậu thấy mấy người lạ.
> Chương 1: «While Thaimi was filling her pot, Angula saw a hare and ran after it.»

- It had sweet fruit for everyone. | Nó có quả ngọt cho mọi người.

- It could talk to people. | Nó biết nói chuyện với người.
> Chương 3: «The trunk was hollow, and there was a lot of space inside.»
