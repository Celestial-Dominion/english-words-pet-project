=== st-c1-the-adventures-of-major-brown 1
? What was Rupert Grant's job? | Rupert Grant làm nghề gì?
+ He was a private detective. | Anh là thám tử tư.
- He was a famous judge. | Anh là một thẩm phán nổi tiếng.
- He was a retired soldier. | Anh là một quân nhân về hưu.
- He was a flower seller. | Anh là người bán hoa.
> Chương 1: «His younger brother Rupert was a far more pragmatic man, who had been a journalist, a teacher and an inventor, and was now a private detective.»

? What was Major Brown's one great passion? | Niềm đam mê lớn duy nhất của Thiếu tá Brown là gì?
+ Growing pansies | Trồng hoa păng-xê
- Collecting old swords | Sưu tầm gươm cổ
- Telling war stories | Kể chuyện chiến tranh
- Reading detective novels | Đọc tiểu thuyết trinh thám
> Chương 1: «He was methodical to an extraordinary degree, precise in his dress and habits, and his one great passion was the cultivation of pansies.»

? What was unusual about the way the Major spoke? | Cách nói của Thiếu tá có gì khác thường?
+ He said only a quarter of each sentence. | Ông chỉ nói một phần tư mỗi câu.
- He always spoke in a whisper. | Ông luôn nói thì thầm.
- He repeated every word twice. | Ông lặp lại mỗi từ hai lần.
- He spoke only about the weather. | Ông chỉ nói về thời tiết.
> Chương 1: «"Yellow pansies. Coal cellar. Death to me. Preposterous," said the Major, who had a quirky habit of speaking only a quarter of each sentence.»

=== st-c1-the-adventures-of-major-brown 2
? Why did the Major climb onto the wall? | Vì sao Thiếu tá trèo lên bức tường?
+ He was told there were fine pansies there. | Ông được bảo trong đó có păng-xê đẹp.
- He heard someone calling for help. | Ông nghe thấy ai đó kêu cứu.
- He wanted to escape from the flower seller. | Ông muốn thoát khỏi người bán hoa.
- He was looking for his lost hat. | Ông đang tìm chiếc mũ bị mất.
> Chương 2: «"If you're interested in pansies, sir," said the man, "just get up on that wall. There's the finest show of yellow pansies in the country in that garden."»

? What did the Major see in the garden? | Thiếu tá thấy gì trong khu vườn?
+ Pansies spelling out a threat | Păng-xê xếp thành một lời đe dọa
- A soldier from his old regiment | Một người lính trong trung đoàn cũ của ông
- A garden full of dead flowers | Một khu vườn đầy hoa đã chết
- A lady waiting for him at the gate | Một quý cô đang đợi ông ở cổng
> Chương 2: «In the middle of the lawn, a great bed of pansies had been planted in gigantic letters, and they spelled out the words "Death to Major Brown".»

? Why did the Major think the garden could not be a joke? | Vì sao Thiếu tá nghĩ khu vườn không thể là trò đùa?
+ It must have cost too much money. | Nó hẳn đã tốn quá nhiều tiền.
- He had no friends who liked jokes. | Ông không có bạn nào thích đùa.
- The flowers were all of his favorite color. | Hoa đều mang màu ông ưa thích.
- The old man looked very serious. | Ông lão trông rất nghiêm nghị.
> Chương 2: «Another man might have doubted his own sanity, but the Major knew that such an elaborate garden must have cost a great deal, and the extravagance of it made a joke seem implausible.»

=== st-c1-the-adventures-of-major-brown 3
? Why did the lady keep her face turned to the street? | Vì sao quý cô cứ quay mặt ra đường?
+ She had to do so until the clock struck six. | Cô phải làm thế cho đến khi đồng hồ điểm sáu giờ.
- She was waiting for her husband to come home. | Cô đang chờ chồng về.
- She did not want the Major to see her face. | Cô không muốn Thiếu tá thấy mặt mình.
- She was watching the man with the cart. | Cô đang theo dõi người đẩy xe hoa.
> Chương 3: «"Until the clock strikes six, I must keep my face turned to the street," she explained, and the Major, who was beginning to enjoy the mystery, accepted this without surprise.»

? Where was the voice in the street coming from? | Giọng nói ngoài đường phát ra từ đâu?
+ A man's head sticking out of the coal-hole | Đầu một người đàn ông thò lên từ lỗ đổ than
- A window on the top floor of the house | Một ô cửa sổ trên tầng cao nhất
- A cab waiting at the end of the street | Một chiếc xe ngựa chờ ở cuối phố
- A parrot in a cage by the door | Một con vẹt trong lồng cạnh cửa
> Chương 3: «On the third time he saw the truth: a man's head was sticking out of the coal-hole in the pavement, and it vanished at once.»

? What did the Major find when he came back up from the cellar? | Thiếu tá thấy gì khi từ dưới hầm trở lên?
+ The house was completely empty. | Ngôi nhà trống trơn hoàn toàn.
- The lady was waiting with the police. | Quý cô đang chờ cùng cảnh sát.
- The old man was watering the pansies. | Ông lão đang tưới păng-xê.
- Rupert and Basil had arrived. | Rupert và Basil đã đến.
> Chương 3: «When Brown climbed back up the stairs, the lady, the curtains and every piece of furniture had disappeared, and the house was bare and empty.»

=== st-c1-the-adventures-of-major-brown 4
? What did the letter in the coat ask Mr. Plover to do? | Lá thư trong áo khoác yêu cầu ông Plover làm gì?
+ Attack the Major in the coal cellar | Tấn công Thiếu tá trong hầm than
- Sell the house to the Major | Bán ngôi nhà cho Thiếu tá
- Water the pansies every day | Tưới păng-xê mỗi ngày
- Pay a bill to Northover | Trả một hóa đơn cho Northover
> Chương 4: «"Dear Mr. Plover, I am annoyed to hear that there has been some delay in the arrangements regarding Major Brown. Please see that he is attacked tomorrow, as arranged. The coal cellar, of course. Yours sincerely, P. G. Northover."»

? What did Basil think about the letter? | Basil nghĩ gì về lá thư?
+ It was not a criminal's letter. | Đó không phải thư của tội phạm.
- It had been written by the lady in green. | Nó do quý cô áo xanh viết.
- It was clear proof of a serious crime. | Nó là bằng chứng rõ ràng của một tội nghiêm trọng.
- It had been written many years earlier. | Nó được viết từ nhiều năm trước.
> Chương 4: «Basil, who had been staring into the fire, spoke quietly. "I don't think it's the sort of letter one criminal would write to another."»

? Why was Basil shaking as they reached the office? | Vì sao Basil run lên khi họ đến văn phòng?
+ He was laughing silently. | Ông đang cười thầm.
- He was afraid of the criminals. | Ông sợ bọn tội phạm.
- He was very cold. | Ông rất lạnh.
- He was angry with Rupert. | Ông giận Rupert.
> Chương 4: «Then he realized, with considerable irritation, that Basil was laughing silently to himself.»

=== st-c1-the-adventures-of-major-brown 6
? What does the Adventure and Romance Agency do? | Hãng Phiêu lưu và Lãng mạn làm gì?
+ It creates adventures for people who pay for them. | Nó tạo ra những cuộc phiêu lưu cho người trả tiền.
- It sells rare flowers to collectors. | Nó bán hoa quý cho dân sưu tầm.
- It helps the police to catch criminals. | Nó giúp cảnh sát bắt tội phạm.
- It publishes adventure novels. | Nó xuất bản tiểu thuyết phiêu lưu.
> Chương 6: «"Our subscribers pay us a regular sum, and in return we surround them with mysteries, chases and strange visitors, all designed to give them the exhilaration of a real adventure."»

? Why had the Major been caught up in the adventure? | Vì sao Thiếu tá bị cuốn vào cuộc phiêu lưu?
+ The clerks confused him with Gurney-Brown. | Nhân viên đã nhầm ông với Gurney-Brown.
- He had paid for it without knowing. | Ông đã trả tiền mà không biết.
- Basil had arranged it as a joke. | Basil đã sắp đặt nó như một trò đùa.
- Miss Jameson wanted to meet him. | Cô Jameson muốn gặp ông.
> Chương 6: «"Mr. Gurney-Brown was one of our subscribers, and our foolish clerks thought that he and you were the same person."»

? Why did Miss Jameson admire the Major? | Vì sao cô Jameson ngưỡng mộ Thiếu tá?
+ He bravely went down into the cellar. | Ông dũng cảm xuống hầm.
- He was the best actor in the agency. | Ông là diễn viên giỏi nhất của hãng.
- He paid the whole bill at once. | Ông trả toàn bộ hóa đơn ngay lập tức.
- He had won a medal for growing pansies. | Ông từng đoạt huy chương nhờ trồng păng-xê.
> Chương 6: «She liked to say that she had met many men who acted bravely in Northover's little dramas, but only one who had gone down into a cellar when he truly believed that a murderer was waiting there.»
