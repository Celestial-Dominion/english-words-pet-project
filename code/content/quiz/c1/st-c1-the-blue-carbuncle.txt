=== st-c1-the-blue-carbuncle 1
? How had Peterson come to have the hat and goose? | Vì sao Peterson có được chiếc mũ và con ngỗng?
+ A stranger dropped them during a struggle. | Một người lạ đánh rơi chúng trong vụ xô xát.
- A friend gave them to him as a present. | Một người bạn tặng anh làm quà.
- He bought them at a market stall. | Anh mua chúng ở một sạp chợ.
- He found them outside Holmes's door. | Anh thấy chúng trước cửa nhà Holmes.
> Chương 1: «and in the chaos the stranger had dropped both his hat and his bird and fled.»

? Why did Holmes think the owner was intelligent? | Vì sao Holmes nghĩ chủ chiếc mũ thông minh?
+ The hat was very large. | Chiếc mũ rất to.
- The hat was very expensive. | Chiếc mũ rất đắt.
- The hat had a name inside. | Bên trong mũ có tên.
- The hat was clean and new. | Chiếc mũ sạch và mới.
> Chương 1: «The owner was a man of considerable intellect, since the hat was very large»

? What did the candle wax suggest? | Vết sáp nến gợi ý điều gì?
+ He had no gas lighting at home. | Nhà ông không có đèn khí.
- He worked late in a church. | Ông làm việc khuya ở nhà thờ.
- His wife no longer loved him. | Vợ ông không còn yêu ông.
- He was very poor and old. | Ông rất nghèo và già.
> Chương 1: «and he had no gas lighting at home, because the hat was spotted with candle wax.»

=== st-c1-the-blue-carbuncle 2

- At the Hotel Cosmopolitan | Ở khách sạn Cosmopolitan

? Who had been arrested for the theft? | Ai đã bị bắt vì vụ trộm?

+ Placed an advertisement for Henry Baker | Đăng quảng cáo tìm Henry Baker
- Took the stone straight to the police | Mang viên đá thẳng đến cảnh sát
- Visited the Countess of Morcar at home | Đến nhà thăm nữ bá tước xứ Morcar
- Questioned the staff at the hotel | Thẩm vấn nhân viên khách sạn
> Chương 2: «Holmes immediately placed an advertisement in all the evening newspapers, inviting Mr. Henry Baker to collect his hat and goose at Baker Street that evening.»

=== st-c1-the-blue-carbuncle 3
? Why had Mr. Baker not advertised for his property? | Vì sao ông Baker không đăng báo tìm đồ?
+ Money was scarcer for him than before. | Tiền bạc của ông eo hẹp hơn trước.
- He thought the police had it. | Ông nghĩ cảnh sát đã giữ chúng.
- He did not read the newspapers. | Ông không đọc báo.
- He had left the city for a week. | Ông đã rời thành phố một tuần.
> Chương 3: «He explained, with some embarrassment, that he had not advertised for his property because money was scarcer for him than it once had been.»

? How did Holmes know that Baker was innocent? | Làm sao Holmes biết Baker vô tội?
+ He didn't care about the old bird's remains. | Ông chẳng quan tâm phần còn lại của con ngỗng cũ.
- He was at home on the day of the theft. | Hôm xảy ra vụ trộm ông ở nhà.
- He answered every question honestly. | Ông trả lời mọi câu hỏi thành thật.
- Peterson recognized his face at once. | Peterson nhận ra mặt ông ngay.
> Chương 3: «His lack of interest was the test Holmes had planned: a guilty man would have been desperate to examine the remains, so Baker was clearly innocent.»

? Where had Baker got the goose? | Baker có con ngỗng từ đâu?
+ Through a goose club at an inn | Qua một câu lạc bộ ngỗng ở quán trọ
- From his wife's family farm | Từ trang trại nhà vợ
- At a shop near the museum | Ở một cửa hàng gần bảo tàng
- As a present from a friend | Là quà của một người bạn
> Chương 3: «Before leaving, Baker mentioned that he had obtained the goose through a goose club at the Alpha Inn, near the British Museum.»

=== st-c1-the-blue-carbuncle 4

- A market in another city | Một khu chợ ở thành phố khác

? What did Holmes and Watson hear as they walked away? | Holmes và Watson nghe thấy gì khi bỏ đi?

- Breckinridge laughing loudly at them | Breckinridge cười to chế giễu họ
- A policeman calling Holmes's name | Một cảnh sát gọi tên Holmes
- The landlord shouting for help | Chủ quán kêu cứu
> Chương 4: «Just then they heard an argument behind them: a small, nervous man was begging Breckinridge for information about the very same geese.»

=== st-c1-the-blue-carbuncle 5

=== st-c1-the-blue-carbuncle 6
? What did Holmes do with Ryder? | Holmes xử lý Ryder thế nào?

- He made him pay a fine. | Anh bắt hắn nộp phạt.
- He sent him to the Countess. | Anh giải hắn đến gặp nữ bá tước.

? What reason did Holmes give for his mercy? | Holmes đưa ra lý do gì cho sự khoan dung của mình?

> Holmes nói: «Besides, it is the season of forgiveness.»
