=== st-c1-the-garden-party 1
? Why did Laura go out to meet the workmen? | Vì sao Laura ra gặp đám thợ?
+ Her sisters were not ready, and she was the artistic one. | Các chị chưa sẵn sàng, và cô là người có khiếu nghệ thuật.
- Her mother had ordered her to supervise them. | Mẹ cô ra lệnh cho cô giám sát họ.
- She knew the tall workman from school. | Cô quen người thợ cao lớn từ hồi đi học.
- She wanted to complain about the noise. | Cô muốn phàn nàn về tiếng ồn.
> Chương 1: «Meg had just washed her hair, and Jose was still in her silk dressing gown, so it was Laura, the artistic one, who ran out to meet the workmen, still holding a piece of bread and butter.»

? What did the tall workman do that delighted Laura? | Người thợ cao lớn đã làm gì khiến Laura thích thú?
+ He picked some lavender and smelled it with pleasure. | Anh ngắt chút oải hương và ngửi nó đầy khoan khoái.
- He offered to carry her bread and butter. | Anh đề nghị cầm giúp lát bánh mì của cô.
- He praised her mother's beautiful garden. | Anh khen khu vườn đẹp của mẹ cô.
- He asked her to dance at the party. | Anh mời cô khiêu vũ ở bữa tiệc.
> Chương 1: «Laura was sorry to hide the lovely trees, but she noticed something that delighted her: the tall man had bent down, picked a small piece of lavender and was smelling it with obvious pleasure.»

? What did Laura blame for keeping people like her apart from the workmen? | Laura đổ lỗi cho điều gì khiến người như cô xa cách những người thợ?
+ Absurd class distinctions | Những phân biệt giai cấp phi lý
- Her mother's strict rules | Những quy tắc nghiêm khắc của mẹ cô
- The workmen's bad manners | Cách cư xử kém của những người thợ
- The silly boys she danced with | Những cậu trai ngớ ngẩn cô khiêu vũ cùng
> Chương 1: «It was all the fault of these absurd, elitist class distinctions, she decided; for her part, she did not feel them at all.»

=== st-c1-the-garden-party 2
? Why had Mrs. Sheridan ordered so many lilies? | Vì sao bà Sheridan đặt nhiều hoa huệ đến thế?
+ For once she wanted enough of them. | Một lần trong đời bà muốn có đủ hoa.
- The florist had made a mistake. | Người bán hoa đã nhầm lẫn.
- Laura had asked her to buy them. | Laura đã nhờ bà mua chúng.
- They were cheaper than roses. | Chúng rẻ hơn hoa hồng.
> Chương 2: «"It's quite right," said Mrs. Sheridan calmly. "For once in my life I wanted enough lilies, and the party is a good excuse."»

? How did Jose treat the preparations for the party? | Jose coi việc chuẩn bị tiệc như thế nào?
+ Like a theatrical performance | Như một vở diễn sân khấu
- Like a boring duty | Như một bổn phận nhàm chán
- Like a serious business | Như một việc làm ăn nghiêm túc
- Like a waste of money | Như sự phí tiền
> Chương 2: «Meanwhile the drawing room was cleared with conscientious care, and Jose, who loved giving orders to the servants, treated the whole affair like a theatrical performance.»

? What did Laura and Jose do with the cream puffs? | Laura và Jose đã làm gì với những chiếc bánh su kem?
+ They ate some of them. | Họ ăn vài chiếc.
- They refused to touch them. | Họ không chịu đụng tới.
- They sent them back to the bakery. | Họ gửi trả chúng về tiệm bánh.
- They gave them to the workmen. | Họ đem cho đám thợ.
> Chương 2: «Laura and Jose felt far too grown-up to care about such things, yet two minutes later they were licking cream from their fingers.»

=== st-c1-the-garden-party 3
? What had happened to the young carter? | Chuyện gì đã xảy ra với anh đánh xe trẻ?
+ He was thrown from his cart and killed. | Anh bị hất khỏi xe và chết.
- He had lost his horse in the town. | Anh làm mất con ngựa trong thị trấn.
- He had fallen ill with a fever. | Anh lên cơn sốt.
- He had been arrested for stealing. | Anh bị bắt vì ăn trộm.
> Chương 3: «The man from Godber's explained that a young carter named Scott, who lived in one of the little cottages at the bottom of the hill, had been thrown from his cart that morning when his horse took fright, and he told the story with tactless enthusiasm.»

? What did Laura want to do after hearing the news? | Laura muốn làm gì sau khi nghe tin?
+ Stop the garden party | Dừng bữa tiệc ngoài vườn
- Visit the hospital | Đến thăm bệnh viện
- Send money to the family | Gửi tiền cho gia đình ấy
- Tell her father at once | Báo ngay cho bố cô
> Chương 3: «Laura, appalled, dragged her sister into the hall. "Jose, however are we going to stop everything?"»

? How did Jose react to Laura's idea? | Jose phản ứng thế nào với ý định của Laura?
+ She thought it was absurd. | Cô cho rằng điều đó thật vô lý.
- She agreed at once. | Cô đồng ý ngay.
- She began to cry. | Cô bắt đầu khóc.
- She went to tell the guests. | Cô đi báo cho khách.
> Chương 3: «"Stop everything?" cried Jose in astonishment. "My dear Laura, don't be so absurd. Nobody expects us to."»

=== st-c1-the-garden-party 4
? What was Mrs. Sheridan's first question when Laura told her the news? | Câu hỏi đầu tiên của bà Sheridan khi Laura báo tin là gì?
+ Whether it had happened in the garden | Có phải chuyện xảy ra trong vườn không
- Whether the man had any children | Người đàn ông có con không
- Whether the band had arrived | Ban nhạc đã đến chưa
- Whether Laura had seen the body | Laura đã thấy thi thể chưa
> Chương 4: «"Mother, a man's been killed," she began breathlessly. "Not in the garden?" interrupted Mrs. Sheridan, and when she heard that it was not, she sighed with relief.»

? How did Mrs. Sheridan stop Laura from arguing? | Bà Sheridan ngăn Laura tranh luận bằng cách nào?
+ She put the new hat on Laura's head. | Bà đội chiếc mũ mới lên đầu Laura.
- She sent Laura to her room. | Bà bắt Laura về phòng.
- She promised to visit the widow. | Bà hứa sẽ đến thăm bà góa.
- She called her husband home. | Bà gọi chồng về nhà.
> Chương 4: «"Mother, isn't it terribly insensitive of us?" asked Laura, but before she could say more, her mother had placed the new hat on Laura's head.»

? What did Laura decide after looking in the mirror? | Laura quyết định gì sau khi nhìn vào gương?
+ To think about it later | Nghĩ về chuyện đó sau
- To go to the cottages at once | Đến những căn nhà nhỏ ngay lập tức
- To give her new hat to the widow | Tặng chiếc mũ mới cho bà góa
- To stay in her room all afternoon | Ở trong phòng suốt buổi chiều
> Chương 4: «For a moment she saw the poor woman and the children again, but the picture seemed blurred and unreal, like a photograph in a newspaper, and she decided to think about it after the party.»

=== st-c1-the-garden-party 5
? What did the guests compare to bright birds? | Điều gì được so sánh với những cánh chim rực rỡ?
+ The guests streaming into the garden | Khách khứa ùa vào khu vườn
- The flowers on the lily lawn | Những bông hoa trên bãi cỏ hoa huệ
- The musicians in the band | Các nhạc công trong ban nhạc
- The girls' colorful dresses | Những chiếc váy sặc sỡ của các cô gái
> Chương 5: «By half past two the band had arrived, and soon guests were streaming into the garden like bright birds that had landed there for a single afternoon.»

? Why was the silence after the party awkward? | Vì sao sự im lặng sau bữa tiệc lại ngượng ngùng?
+ Mr. Sheridan had mentioned the accident. | Ông Sheridan đã nhắc đến vụ tai nạn.
- The guests had left without saying goodbye. | Khách ra về không chào hỏi.
- Laura had refused to eat anything. | Laura không chịu ăn gì.
- The marquee had fallen down. | Chiếc lều tiệc bị sập.
> Chương 5: «An awkward silence fell; it was very tactless of father, thought his wife, but then she had one of her brilliant ideas.»

? What was Mrs. Sheridan's brilliant idea? | Ý tưởng sáng chói của bà Sheridan là gì?
+ To send the widow a basket of food | Gửi cho bà góa một giỏ đồ ăn
- To invite the widow to the next party | Mời bà góa dự bữa tiệc sau
- To pay for the young man's funeral | Trả tiền đám tang cho chàng trai
- To give the lilies to the church | Tặng hoa huệ cho nhà thờ
> Chương 5: «"Let's make up a basket and send that poor creature some of this perfectly good food," she said. "Laura, take it down yourself, just as you are."»

=== st-c1-the-garden-party 6
? How did Laura feel as she walked down the lane? | Laura cảm thấy thế nào khi đi xuống con ngõ?
+ Out of place and stared at | Lạc lõng và bị nhìn chằm chằm
- Proud of her beautiful dress | Tự hào về chiếc váy đẹp
- Happy to help the poor | Vui vì được giúp người nghèo
- Bored and impatient | Chán và sốt ruột
> Chương 6: «She felt horribly out of place, a creature of lavish parties standing in a world she had never tried to understand.»

? What did Laura see in the bedroom? | Laura thấy gì trong phòng ngủ?
+ The young man, peacefully asleep | Chàng trai trẻ như đang ngủ yên
- The children playing on the floor | Lũ trẻ chơi trên sàn
- The widow crying over the basket | Bà góa khóc bên chiếc giỏ
- Her brother waiting for her | Anh trai đang đợi cô
> Chương 6: «He looked as if he were fast asleep, so far away and so peaceful that garden parties, baskets and lace dresses could mean nothing to him, and Laura, inexplicably, felt not horror but wonder.»

? What did Laura say before leaving the room? | Laura nói gì trước khi rời căn phòng?
+ "Forgive my hat." | "Xin thứ lỗi cho chiếc mũ của tôi."
- "I am so sorry." | "Tôi rất tiếc."
- "Mother sent this." | "Mẹ tôi gửi cái này."
- "Please wake up." | "Xin hãy tỉnh dậy."
> Chương 6: «Laura gave a loud, childish sob. "Forgive my hat," she said, and she hurried out of the house and up the dark lane.»
