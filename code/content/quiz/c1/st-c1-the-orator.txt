=== st-c1-the-orator 1
? Why did Poplavsky hurry off to find Zapoikin? | Vì sao Poplavsky vội vã đi tìm Zapoikin?
+ To ask him to make a speech at the funeral | Để nhờ anh đọc diễn văn ở đám tang
- To tell him that his friend had died | Để báo tin bạn anh đã mất
- To invite him to a merchant's wedding | Để mời anh dự đám cưới nhà buôn
- To borrow money for the funeral | Để vay tiền lo đám tang
> Chương 1: «As the funeral procession set off from the church towards the cemetery, one of the deceased's colleagues, a clerk called Poplavsky, jumped into a cab and hurried off to find a friend.»

? What was Zapoikin famous for? | Zapoikin nổi tiếng nhờ điều gì?
+ Making speeches without any preparation | Đọc diễn văn không cần chuẩn bị
- Writing poems for weddings | Viết thơ cho đám cưới
- Organizing funerals for officials | Tổ chức đám tang cho viên chức
- Singing at the city's churches | Hát ở các nhà thờ trong thành phố
> Chương 1: «The friend was Grigory Petrovitch Zapoikin, a young man who had acquired considerable renown for his talent at making speeches without any preparation.»

? What sometimes happened at merchants' weddings? | Điều gì đôi khi xảy ra ở đám cưới nhà buôn?
+ The police were called to stop him talking. | Người ta gọi cảnh sát đến ngăn anh nói.
- He refused to speak without payment. | Anh từ chối nói nếu không được trả tiền.
- The guests asked him to sing instead. | Khách mời bảo anh hát thay vì nói.
- He fell asleep in the middle of a speech. | Anh ngủ gật giữa bài diễn văn.
> Chương 1: «His speeches were full of lavish praise and generous flattery, and at some merchants' weddings he talked so tediously and for so long that the police had to be called to stop him.»

=== st-c1-the-orator 2
? Why did Poplavsky think a speech was necessary? | Vì sao Poplavsky nghĩ cần có một bài diễn văn?
+ The dead man had been an important secretary. | Người chết từng là một thư ký quan trọng.
- The widow had asked for one. | Bà góa đã yêu cầu.
- The law required a speech at every funeral. | Luật bắt buộc mọi đám tang phải có diễn văn.
- Nobody else knew the dead man well. | Không ai khác biết rõ người chết.
> Chương 2: «"If it were some junior subordinate, it wouldn't matter, but this is the secretary, a pillar of the office. It would be awkward to bury such an important man without a speech."»

? What did Zapoikin say about the secretary? | Zapoikin nói gì về ông thư ký?
+ That he was a cunning old rascal | Rằng ông là một lão già láu cá
- That he was the kindest man in the office | Rằng ông là người tốt nhất văn phòng
- That he had never met him | Rằng anh chưa từng gặp ông
- That he was a famous speaker | Rằng ông là một diễn giả nổi tiếng
> Chương 2: «"Oh, the secretary," yawned Zapoikin smugly. "I know him well: a cunning old rascal, if ever there was one."»

? How did Zapoikin prepare himself before going out? | Zapoikin chuẩn bị thế nào trước khi ra ngoài?
+ He arranged his face into an expression of grief. | Anh sắp xếp gương mặt thành vẻ đau buồn.
- He wrote his speech on a piece of paper. | Anh viết bài diễn văn ra giấy.
- He asked Poplavsky about the dead man's life. | Anh hỏi Poplavsky về cuộc đời người chết.
- He put on his best black suit. | Anh mặc bộ com-lê đen đẹp nhất.
> Chương 2: «Zapoikin agreed readily, for he never refused an audience; he ruffled his hair, arranged his face into an expression of somber grief and went out into the street.»

=== st-c1-the-orator 3

- "Who will pay for this?" | "Ai sẽ trả tiền cho việc này?"
- "Where is the speaker?" | "Diễn giả đâu rồi?"
> Chương 3: «When the coffin was lowered into the grave, the widow even cried out, "Let me go with him!", although, perhaps remembering her pension, she stayed exactly where she was.»

? Why did the widow stay where she was, according to the narrator? | Theo người kể, vì sao bà góa vẫn đứng nguyên chỗ cũ?

- Her sister held her back. | Em gái bà giữ bà lại.
- The priest told her to stay calm. | Linh mục bảo bà bình tĩnh.
- She was too tired to move. | Bà quá mệt để cử động.
> Chương 3: «When the coffin was lowered into the grave, the widow even cried out, "Let me go with him!", although, perhaps remembering her pension, she stayed exactly where she was.»

+ Prokofy Osipitch | Prokofy Osipitch
- Kirill Ivanovitch | Kirill Ivanovitch
- Poplavsky | Poplavsky

=== st-c1-the-orator 4
? Which of these did Zapoikin praise in his speech? | Zapoikin ca ngợi điều nào trong bài diễn văn?
+ The dead man's honesty and integrity | Sự trung thực và liêm chính của người chết
- The dead man's skill as a speaker | Tài diễn thuyết của người chết
- The dead man's large fortune | Gia tài lớn của người chết
- The dead man's happy marriage | Cuộc hôn nhân hạnh phúc của người chết
> Chương 4: «"He was dutiful and conscientious, working late into the night, and his integrity was absolute: no bribe could ever tempt him!"»

? Why could the dead man hardly be called a bachelor? | Vì sao khó gọi người chết là người độc thân?
+ He had a wife all his life. | Ông có vợ suốt đời.
- He had been married three times. | Ông đã kết hôn ba lần.
- He lived with his mother. | Ông sống với mẹ.
- He had many children. | Ông có nhiều con.
> Chương 4: «First, the deceased had been called Kirill Ivanovitch, not Prokofy Osipitch; second, he had spent his whole life quarreling with his wife, so he could hardly be called a bachelor.»

? What was wrong with Zapoikin's description of the dead man's face? | Lời tả gương mặt người chết của Zapoikin sai ở đâu?
+ The dead man had a thick red beard. | Người chết có bộ râu đỏ rậm.
- The dead man had very fine features. | Người chết có nét mặt rất thanh tú.
- The dead man wore glasses. | Người chết đeo kính.
- The dead man was very young. | Người chết còn rất trẻ.
> Chương 4: «Third, he had worn a thick red beard all his life, yet the orator kept praising his kind, clean-shaven face.»

=== st-c1-the-orator 5
? Why did Zapoikin stop in the middle of a sentence? | Vì sao Zapoikin ngừng bặt giữa câu?
+ He saw the man he was praising standing nearby. | Anh thấy người mình đang ca ngợi đứng gần đó.
- He forgot what he wanted to say. | Anh quên mất mình định nói gì.
- The widow began to cry loudly. | Bà góa bắt đầu khóc to.
- Poplavsky told him to finish quickly. | Poplavsky bảo anh nói nhanh cho xong.
> Chương 5: «"I say, he's alive!" he whispered to Poplavsky, staring in horror. "Prokofy Osipitch is standing over there by that tombstone!"»

? What did Poplavsky tell Zapoikin to do? | Poplavsky bảo Zapoikin làm gì?
+ Go on with the speech | Tiếp tục bài diễn văn
- Apologize to the widow | Xin lỗi bà góa
- Leave the cemetery at once | Rời nghĩa trang ngay
- Start the speech again | Bắt đầu lại bài diễn văn
> Chương 5: «"Kirill Ivanovitch was our secretary. Prokofy Osipitch had the job before him, but he was moved to another department two years ago. Go on, it's awkward to stop now!"»

? Why was Prokofy Osipitch offended? | Vì sao Prokofy Osipitch phật lòng?
+ The praise sounded like sarcasm. | Lời ca ngợi nghe như mỉa mai.
- Nobody had invited him to the funeral. | Không ai mời ông dự đám tang.
- Zapoikin had forgotten to mention him. | Zapoikin quên nhắc đến ông.
- He had wanted to give a speech himself. | Ông muốn tự mình đọc diễn văn.
> Chương 5: «"Your speech may have been fine for a dead man, young man," he grumbled irately, "but said about a living one, it was nothing but sarcasm."»
